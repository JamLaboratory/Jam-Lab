# The Sunday Jam — editorial and delivery specification

Status: Approved editorial format; email platform, subscription intake and automatic sending are NOT yet configured.
Brand: Jam Laboratory / Jam Lab. Motto: No Good Song Left Behind.
Name: The Sunday Jam.
Header: The approved Classic Listener primary logo (/classic-listener.svg) next to the words “The Sunday Jam.” Use forest green #214B3B, ivory #F5F0E3, antique gold #B69A55. Never substitute an image-search approximation.
Schedule target: Sundays, 8:00 AM America/Chicago.
Coverage: Previous Monday–Sunday for Jam Lab publications and music news, with upcoming Monday–Sunday for releases. At Sunday 8 AM, current Sunday is not complete; use the seven full days ending Saturday for past-week coverage to avoid incomplete-day ambiguity. Record exact date windows in each edition.

## Part I — This Week at Jam Lab
1. Highest-rated and lowest-rated album reviews published during the coverage window, plus one random review from the complete review archive. Include artwork, artist, album, published Jam Lab rating, a short accurate excerpt and review link. If only one new review exists, feature it once and label it accordingly; if none, say no new reviews briefly or omit weekly extremes. Avoid selecting an archive review already featured in the same issue.
2. Most recently published article (not necessarily from the week), with headline, artwork if available, short preview and link; label its actual publication date.
3. All other album reviews published in the week, as a compact gallery with artwork and Jam Lab rating ONLY (no descriptions). Do not repeat the featured highest and lowest reviews.

## Part II — Beyond the Lab
1. Exactly two notable, verified music news stories from the previous week, selected from rock, country, metal, folk, pop, and jazz. Each gets 2–3 lines and a link to the original credible source.
2. Five anticipated albums due for release in the upcoming Monday–Sunday window, chosen from rock, metal, pop, blues, country, folk, jazz, R&B and progressive music. Show artist, album, genre and verified expected release date. Never invent albums or dates; if fewer than five can be verified, publish fewer and do not pad.

## Editorial and operational rules
- Preserve Nick Walsh's independent editorial voice. Do not fabricate Jam Lab content, scores, quotes, listening experiences, or source links.
- Published review scores are derived from five equal-weight criteria: composition, writing, performance, production and intangibles; use the website's scoreFor function.
- If scores tie for high or low, select deterministically (newest first, then slug) and do not feature the same review twice.
- Archive randomization should be stable for a given edition, so a regenerated email does not unexpectedly change its selection.
- Use artwork only from published Jam Lab content, with descriptive alt text.
- First editions require editor approval. Do not automatically send to subscribers until the mailing service, verified sender domain, subscription consent, unsubscribe handling, scheduling, and test sends have been set up and explicitly approved.
- Never put subscriber email addresses in public GitHub files or website source.
- Include sender identification, postal mailing address and unsubscribe link via the chosen mailing provider.
- Track sent editions and prevent duplicate sends for the same Sunday.
- Respect source copyright: brief original summaries and links, not reproduced articles.
